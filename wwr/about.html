Ecuador Rafting Adventures

Our mission is to share the beauty of Ecuador through exciting river adventures. Located in the heart of the Amazon region, we want visitors to experience the excitement of rafting while discovering Ecuador's incredible natural landscapes. Safety, teamwork, and respect for nature are our priorities.

History

Ecuador Rafting Adventures was created with the dream of sharing Ecuador's natural beauty with the world. Inspired by the rivers of Tena, in the province of Napo, our company celebrates the adventure, culture, and biodiversity of the Ecuadorian Amazon. We believe every river journey creates unforgettable memories.
